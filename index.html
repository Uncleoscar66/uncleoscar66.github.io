Jarvis

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Jarvis is a personal voice assistant built for one person, its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service: no one else can sign up for it or use it.

What it does

It answers spoken questions and controls the owner's own media and productivity accounts when the owner asks it to. For example, it can search YouTube, open a video in the owner's browser, list the owner's playlists, or add a video to a playlist the owner names.

Your data

Data stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold or shared, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
